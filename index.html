Notes from the in-between

On poetry, and
why it matters.

Some feelings arrive before we have the words for them. Poetry gives those feelings somewhere to sit. It doesn't always fix a thing; sometimes it simply tells us we are not the only ones who have felt it.

And maybe that is enough. A few honest lines, a page turned slowly, the strange comfort of finding your own heart in someone else's words.

For the things we cannot quite say.
